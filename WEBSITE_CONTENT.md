## Section 3 — Cross-System Workflows (How The Three Apps Talk To Each Other)

### Section Tagline
**Actions Propagate Automatically. No Refresh Required.**

### Section Intro
Because all three SchoolSync apps share one cloud database, an action in one place updates the others in real time. Here are four everyday examples.

### Workflow 1 — Online Fee Payment

A parent pays fees from their phone. Within seconds:

1. Parent App: Payment receipt appears, ready to download as PDF.
2. Admin Panel: Collection number updates on the dashboard; receipt is added to the daily collection register.
3. Teacher App: The class fee-collection counter updates; the student is removed from the defaulter list.
4. All three: Push notification sent to the parent confirming payment.

*The parent never has to share a screenshot. The teacher never has to ask the office. The office never has to reconcile manually.*

### Workflow 2 — Homework Assignment

A teacher assigns homework with a PDF attachment:

1. Teacher App: Compose the homework, attach the file, pick the class. Submit.
2. The file uploads to secure cloud storage; the assignment is recorded in the database.
3. Parent App: Every parent in that class sees the new homework on their dashboard within seconds. They can tap the attachment to download it directly.
4. Admin Panel: The school's principal can monitor homework patterns across classes from the dashboard.

### Workflow 3 — Attendance

A teacher takes morning attendance:

1. Teacher App: Class roster loads, teacher taps Present / Absent / Tardy per student.
2. Within seconds, daily records and the monthly summary update.
3. Parent App: Today's attendance status is visible on the parent's home screen. The monthly calendar reflects the new entry immediately.
4. Admin Panel: The principal sees school-wide attendance percentage on the dashboard.

### Workflow 4 — Behaviour Red Flag

A teacher raises a behaviour concern:

1. Teacher App: Long-press the student, pick category (behaviour / academic / health) and severity, add a note.
2. Parent App: The parent receives a push notification and can see the flag on the Red Flags screen.
3. Admin Panel: The counsellor or principal sees the flag in the school-wide alerts feed for follow-up.

## Section 8 — Why Schools Choose SchoolSync

### Tagline
**Built For Indian Schools. By People Who Know Them.**

### Reason 1 — One Platform, Not Five
You don't need separate software for fees, attendance, communication, and accounting. SchoolSync is one platform with all of it. One login. One vendor. One support team.

### Reason 2 — Your Office Staff Don't Need Re-Training
The Admin Panel works the way your office already works. Print receipts, take admissions, run reports — but now the data is automatically shared with teachers and parents. No new vocabulary to learn.

### Reason 3 — Phased Rollout
You don't have to migrate everything on day one. Start with admissions and fees. Roll out the Teacher app next month. Bring parents on board in month three. We support the gradual approach.

### Reason 4 — Per-School Data Isolation
Multi-tenant architecture means your school's data is completely separate from every other school using SchoolSync. Even if our database server is shared, your data is fenced off at the document level by hardware-enforced security rules.

### Reason 5 — Real-Time, Not Once-A-Day Sync
Many school apps sync data on a schedule — at midnight, every hour, on app open. SchoolSync uses live database listeners. The teacher takes attendance at 9:05 AM; the parent sees it at 9:05 AM.

### Reason 6 — Audit Trail On Everything
Every administrative action — every fee receipt, every mark entered, every promotion, every certificate issued — is logged with who, when, and what changed. Disputes resolve themselves.

### Reason 7 — Offline-First Mobile Apps
Both the Teacher and Parent apps work without a network. They sync when connectivity returns. Crucial in semi-urban schools where Wi-Fi is patchy.

### Reason 8 — Your Data Is Yours
No vendor lock-in. We provide full Excel and PDF exports of every dataset at any time. If you ever leave SchoolSync, you take your data with you.

## Section 9 — Security & Trust

### Tagline
**Your School's Data Is Treated Like Your Bank's Data.**

### Body Copy
SchoolSync runs on Google Firebase — the same infrastructure that powers some of the world's largest mobile applications. We don't manage your data on a server in our office; it lives on Google's enterprise-grade cloud with redundancy, automated backups, and end-to-end encryption.

### Security Features
- **Encrypted in transit.** All data between apps and the cloud uses HTTPS / TLS.
- **Encrypted at rest.** Stored data is encrypted on disk by the cloud provider.
- **Tenant isolation.** Every read and write is gated by your school's unique ID at the database level.
- **Role-based access.** Users see only the modules and data their role permits.
- **Audit trail.** Every important action is logged with timestamps and user IDs.
- **Force password change on first login.** New accounts cannot use default passwords.
- **PCI-DSS-compliant payments.** Card data never touches our system — Razorpay handles all payment processing.
- **Per-school backups.** Schedulable, restorable, exportable.

## Section 13 — Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: We already use [other software] for accounting. Can SchoolSync integrate with it?
We can export accounting data in Tally-compatible XML, generic Excel, and standard CSV formats. Direct two-way integration is on our 2027 roadmap.

### Q: Does SchoolSync work on iPhone?
The Parent and Teacher apps are currently Android-only. iOS versions are planned for late 2026 once we reach the scale that justifies the additional maintenance.

### Q: What happens if Firebase goes down?
Both mobile apps are offline-first — they continue to function and queue actions locally. The Admin Panel is the only piece that requires network. Firebase's reported uptime is 99.95%.

### Q: Where is our data stored?
On Google Firebase servers. Google offers data-residency options; for Indian schools, we can configure storage in Asia-South1 (Mumbai) region.

### Q: Can we export everything if we leave?
Yes. Complete Excel and PDF exports of every module are available on demand. We will never hold your data hostage.

### Q: How long does it take to go live?
Five weeks from contract signing to fully operational, including teacher and parent rollout.

### Q: Can parents use SchoolSync without a smartphone?
The Parent app is required for the best experience. Parents without smartphones can request printed copies of receipts, attendance reports, and marks from the school office.

### Q: Is there a per-user fee?
No. Pricing is per-student-per-year. All staff and parent accounts are included.
