### 5. The data is yours. Always.
Export everything to Excel, PDF, or open formats at any time. We never sell data, never serve advertising, never make leaving harder than joining. The school is the controller; we are the processor.

### 6. We build for the next decade, not the next quarter.
We turn down feature requests that compromise the architecture. We rewrite when we find a better foundation. We treat the codebase like a building that will outlive its first occupants.

## Section W2 — The Ten Reasons

**1. Section Name:** Ten Reasons Grid

**2. Headline:**
**Why institutions choose [Brand].**

**3. Subheadline:**
*(None)*

**4. Body Content:**

### Reason 1 — Real-time, not batch
We don't sync at midnight. Every action — a parent's fee payment, a teacher's attendance mark, a principal's notice — propagates within a second. The era of "the data refreshes overnight" is over.

### Reason 2 — Three native applications, not one repurposed portal
Most competitors ship one product and bolt on a mobile companion. [Brand] ships three first-class, purpose-built applications — web admin, Android teacher, Android parent — engineered around the people who use them, not around a single underlying framework.

### Reason 3 — Offline-first mobile
The Teacher App takes attendance in a basement classroom with no signal. The Parent App reads cached data when the train enters a tunnel. The sync happens automatically when connectivity returns. We engineered for real Indian network conditions, not idealised demos.

### Reason 4 — Multi-tenant by architecture
Every institution is a fully isolated tenant. School chains and multi-branch trusts get a super-admin layer above the branch tenants — consolidated reporting, branch isolation, role-based visibility, all enforced at the database level.

### Reason 5 — Audit-trail-first design
Every administrative change to every record is logged with actor, timestamp, and before/after values. Disputes resolve themselves. Board inspections complete in hours, not weeks. Statutory audits land with everything ready.

### Reason 6 — No vendor lock-in
Export every dataset to Excel, PDF, or CSV on demand. We are processors; you are the data controller. Account closure is a one-click request with a 30-day verification window. Your data is always yours.

### Reason 7 — Migration is on us
Bring us your Excel sheets, your Tally exports, your legacy ERP backups, your paper registers. We extract, transform, validate, and import. Most institutions are fully migrated within 5 weeks of contract signing.

### Reason 8 — Teacher dignity by design
Quiet hours enforced by default. Personal phone numbers never shared with parents. Surveillance-free in-app activity. We refused to build management surveillance into the product — and we say no to schools that ask for it.

### Reason 9 — Built on Google Cloud
Bank-grade encryption, 99.95% uptime, automated per-school backups, redundant storage, data residency options for India. No on-premise server room to maintain. No data trapped in someone's laptop.

### Reason 10 — A product team that ships
We ship every quarter. Bug reports from teachers and parents land in front of engineers, not in a CRM ticket purgatory. Our changelog is public. Our roadmap is public. Our customers are partners in the product, not buyers of a contract.

## 4.4 Testimonial Display Rules

When using these testimonials on the site:

- **Always include the institution name and city.** Generic "happy customer" attributions feel fake.

Google Cloud Firestore. By default, Asia-South1 (Mumbai region) for Indian institutions. International institutions can be configured to use other regions.

Your institution owns the data. [Brand] is the data processor; the institution is the controller. We never sell data, never advertise against it, never share with third parties beyond what's strictly required to deliver the service.

We have a perpetual export commitment in every contract: data is available for export at any time, including after termination. Multi-year customers also have an escrow arrangement available where critical code and data structures are held by a third-party for business-continuity protection.

Yes. We are fully aligned with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Our DPA (Data Processing Agreement) is available on request.

**Q. What about child safety? Can a teacher message my child directly?**
No. Teachers communicate with parents, not directly with children. All teacher-parent communication is logged for child-protection compliance. The institution's administration can review conversations.

**Q. How are payments secured?**
Razorpay processes all card and UPI payments under PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance. Neither [Brand] nor your school ever sees raw card data. Webhooks are signature-validated before any state change.

**Q. Has [Brand] had any data breaches?**
No. Zero data breaches since founding. We publish a security posture page and our incident response plan; both are linked from /security.

Native Android using Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. We don't use cross-platform frameworks (React Native, Flutter) because the performance and platform-API integration matters at the scale we operate.

Late 2026. We have an active design and engineering investment; the timeline is conservative because we won't ship a half-working iOS app.

Yes — Tally-compatible XML export is supported. Two-way integration with specific accounting platforms is on the 2027 roadmap.

We integrate with the major fingerprint, RFID, and facial-recognition vendors used in Indian institutions. Bring us your existing devices; we'll handle integration.

99.95% measured monthly, backed by Google Cloud Firestore's SLA. We've exceeded this every quarter since founding.
