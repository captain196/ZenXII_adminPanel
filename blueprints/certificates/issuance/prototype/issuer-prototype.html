How the register match works, and where the data came from

hpbose.org/Admin/Upload/HPBoSE.Affiliation.List.pdf — 84 pages, ~14,700 rows, a real text layer. Fetched, parsed with PyMuPDF, and 53 rows embedded here verbatim with their affiliation code, school code, name, district, PIN, recognised class range and session. Matching a typed affiliation number against that register is the same act a human reviewer performs — which is why it counts as verification rather than a pretence of it. One row reads NOC TO CBSE: the register even tracks a school leaving for another board.

Prototype only — nothing here writes to ZenXii. CBSE deliberately has no register path: its affiliated-schools page returned HTTP 403, so the honest fallback is shown instead. Behaviour follows ISSUER_UX.md; citations C‑nn refer to the conflict register.
