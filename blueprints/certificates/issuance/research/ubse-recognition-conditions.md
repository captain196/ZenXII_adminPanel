## 1 · Recognition is granted PER STREAM AND PER SUBJECT — and each needs its own track record

This is the strongest per-section recognition evidence in the corpus, and it settles it twice over:
**through the fee schedule and through the evidence burden.**

**Reg 3(ख), fees [A]:**

| | |
|---|---|
| first-time Intermediate exam recognition | **₹10,000** |
| recognition in **any additional वर्ग** (stream) | **₹5,000** |
| one-time recognition for the Intermediate exam | **₹10,000 per वर्ग** |
| **per subject** | **₹2,500, minimum ₹5,000** |

**Reg 5(ढ), evidence [A]:** new Intermediate recognition requires the **last two years' High School
results** with the percentage of candidates appeared and passed. And *"उसी प्रकार इण्टरमीडिएट अतिरिक्त
वर्ग/विषय की मान्यता हेतु विद्यालय के विगत दो वर्षों का इण्टरमीडिएट परीक्षा वर्षवार पृथक-2 परीक्षाफल
दिया जाना आवश्यक होगा"* — **an additional stream or subject needs the school's own last two years of
Intermediate results, year by year, separately.**

**So a school is not "UBSE-recognised".** It is recognised for a stage, for particular streams, and
for particular subjects, each acquired separately, each priced separately, and each requiring its own
two-year performance history. **C-14 held that per-section recognition does not generalise and was
confirmed in only 2 of 8 southern states. This is a northern state establishing it at [A], on two
independent mechanisms.**

Scope limit stated in the document itself: new/additional Intermediate streams are recognised
**केवल छः विषयों में** — in six subjects only.

## 2 · Recognition is withdrawable on a false particular, with personal criminal liability

**Reg 5(थ) [A]** requires the manager to file an affidavit on **₹10 stamp paper**, in a form the
regulation reproduces. Its operative sentence:

> *"आवेदन पत्र के साथ संलग्नकों अथवा आवेदन पत्र में अंकित विवरण/साक्ष्य के असत्य पाये जाने पर
> परिषद्/शासन द्वारा प्रदत्त की गयी मान्यता को प्रत्याहरित किया जा सकता है तथा मेरे विरूद्ध भारतीय दण्ड
> संहिता 1860 के प्रावधानों के अन्तर्गत जो विधिक कार्यवाही की जायेगी मुझे मान्य होगी।"*

**If any annexure or particular is found false, the recognition already granted may be WITHDRAWN, and
the manager accepts legal action against himself under the Indian Penal Code 1860.**

This is **C-4 confirmed on a new instrument** — recognition lapses on *events*, not only on dates —
and it is the sharpest instance found, because the triggering event is **a false statement in the
school's own application**, and the consequence reaches a named individual personally.

**Consequence for the issuer ladder.** `Issuer_identity`'s `EVIDENCED` rung means "the affiliation
instrument is on file." Uttarakhand shows that an instrument on file is **not** proof of a subsisting
entitlement: the same instrument is revocable retroactively on a defect in how it was obtained. The
ladder's refusal to treat a document as proof of truth is the right shape — this is evidence for it,
not against it.

## 3 · Three conditions nobody in the programme had anticipated

| condition | text | why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Hindi medium only** | Reg 8: *"संस्थाओं को मान्यता केवल हिन्दी माध्यम से शिक्षण हेतु प्रदत्त की जायेगी"* **[A]** | A UBSE school's recognition is **medium-bound**. An English-medium claim against UBSE recognition is outside what the Board grants. |
| **No direct 11–12 recognition** | Reg 9: *"किसी भी संस्था को सीधे कक्षा 11 व 12 की मान्यता प्रदान नहीं की जायेगी"* **[A]** | Recognition is **stage-laddered** — a school cannot begin at the senior stage. Explains why Reg 5(ढ) can demand a High School result history. |
| **Current society registration** | Reg (क)1: registered under the Societies Registration Act **and renewal current** **[A]** | The **society's** standing is a live component of the school's recognition, not background paperwork. |

## 4 · The procedure, for the record

**Reg 3(क)** — application on the prescribed form, signed by the **संस्थाध्यक्ष/प्रबन्धक**, to reach
the **परिषद् सचिव** by **31 July** of the year preceding the proposed opening; two copies to the
**मुख्य शिक्षा अधिकारी**. Page 1 gives the High School/Intermediate variant, routing through the
**जिला शिक्षा अधिकारी** of the district with a treasury-challan photocopy, one copy plus the original
challan to the Council Secretary. Fee head **0202–01–102–10**.

**Reg 3(ग)** — after 31 July, accepted only with a **₹1,000 late fee** per calendar month or part;
**after 31 October, not accepted in any circumstance**. **Reg 3(घ)** — incomplete applications get no
action. **Reg 3(ड)** — state-maintained institutions are exempt from application and late fees.

**Reg 4(क)** — the CEO conducts a **स्थलीय जांच**, records his आख्या and संस्तुति on one copy **by 31
October**, and forwards it to the Council Secretary. **Reg 4(ग)** — before the **मान्यता समिति**
sees an application, the Director or a nominee **not below मण्डलीय अपर शिक्षा निदेशक** may add a
report and recommendation.

**Reg 5(ण)** — the inspection includes **photographs from all four directions** attached to the
report, plus a photograph of the boundary wall. **Reg 7** — the CEO must state explicitly whether
recognition should be granted and whether the school is rural or urban, and **the inspecting officer
signs every page**.

**Reg 5(ड)** — the institution must fully adopt **विद्यालयी शिक्षा अधिनियम 2006, धारा 12(1)**.
**Reg 5(च)** — land/building in the school's name, with certified **registry and खतौनी** copies or a
**परगनाधिकारी/तहसीलदार** certificate. **Reg 5(ज)** — proof of **प्राभूत कोष** and **सुरक्षित कोष**
deposited and mortgaged.
