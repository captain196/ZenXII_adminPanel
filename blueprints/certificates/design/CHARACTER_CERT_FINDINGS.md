# Character / Conduct certificate — layout findings

**Status:** research input for starter templates (P8.3). **Weak evidence — read the caveat.**
**Date:** 2026-08-19

## ⚠ Caveat that governs everything below

The research pass could **not verify a single certificate from a real, named Indian school**.
Every source fetched was a template-aggregator or edu-content site (Vedantu, A Plus Topper,
Korangle, FlexiPrep, Eduyush). This is *"how the template industry writes it"* — useful for the
range of variants, **not evidence of what a school's actual stationery looks like**.

Blocked: testbook.com (403), scribd.com (body unreachable), getmyuni.com (images only).

## Layout facts (from fetched pages only)

| Aspect | Finding |
|---|---|
| Heading | **Salutation is as common as a title.** `To Whomsoever It May Concern` / `To Whom It May Concern` in 2 of 4; caps `CHARACTER CERTIFICATE` in 1; title case in 1. Both conventions are live. |
| Ref. / Sl. No. | **Absent entirely** from all four templates. Unlike the TC, this document appears to carry no serial. |
| Date | Bottom, inside the signature block, in all four. Top-of-page dating not verified. |
| Signatories | **One.** No template showed a class-teacher counter-signature. |
| Signatory label | `Name of Principal` · `Principal's Signature & Seal` · `Signature of Principal/Headmaster:` · generic `(Name of the Issuing Authority)` / `(Designation)`. "Headmistress" not found. |
| Seal | Always bottom, adjacent to or beneath the signature. One source distinguishes **signature-stamp** from **school round seal** as separate marks. |
| Photograph | **Not found** in any fetched template. |
| Footer (validity / "computer generated" / QR) | **Not found.** No evidence at all. |
| Letterhead vs pre-printed border | **Unresolved.** Indirect evidence leans letterhead (templates ask for school address *beneath* the signature, which you only do when the sheet has no printed masthead), but no scan settles it. |

## Conduct grading vocabulary

The enumerated set actually found is **Excellent / Good / Satisfactory** — *not* the
Good/Very Good/Excellent scale often assumed.

## Prose patterns (verbatim, from the fetched pages)

- `"It is to certify that <name>, aged <n> years, <son/daughter> of <parent>, who has studied in
  this School from <yyyy> to <yyyy> holds a good moral character."`
- `"This is to certify that <name> was a student of Class X in our school from <yyyy> to <yyyy>.
  He has maintained good conduct and bears a good moral character."`
- `"During this period, their conduct and behavior were found to be [Excellent/Good/Satisfactory].
  We wish them success in all future endeavors."`

Two idioms recur: **"bears a good moral character"** and **"holds a good moral character"**.

## ⛔ Do NOT use — unverified

- `"has not been involved in any anti-social or indisciplinary activity"` — appeared only in a
  search snippet, never in a fetched body.
- SBI-style employment wording — a **bank/employer** form, not a school one.

## Board position

**No prescribed character-certificate format exists at CBSE.** CBSE prescribes the *Transfer*
Certificate (Examination Bye-Laws Annexure-I), which contains a *conduct* field — but nothing
standalone. CISCE unchecked.

This is consistent with our own research grading Character/Conduct as **Level B/C** — the only
prescribed form we hold is Tamil Nadu Educational Rules Appendix 5-B.

## Recommended next step

Stop searching the SEO layer; it is saturated with template farms. Target real school domains:
`filetype:pdf "character certificate" site:*.kvs.gov.in`, Navodaya/DAV/state-board school
"Downloads / Forms" pages. That is where serial placement, photo boxes and footer text would
actually be visible.
