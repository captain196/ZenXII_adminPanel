## 1. Transfer Certificate — CLASSIC  **[measured]**

Source: Springdale Public School TC format. Page verified **A4 exactly** (595.44 × 841.68 pt),
margins ~20 mm L/R, ~25 mm top.

| Aspect | Finding |
|---|---|
| Letterhead | Sheet is **bare** — the format assumes pre-printed school stationery above it. Where a school prints its own, CBSE **requires** the line `AFFILIATED TO THE CENTRAL BOARD OF SECONDARY EDUCATION` plus the Affiliation Code below the name/address (Affiliation Bye-Laws). |
| Title | `TRANSFER CERTIFICATE`, centred, caps, **serif**, ~13–14 pt against ~11 pt body. No underline, no box. |
| Serial block | One line under the title, above field 1: `Book No. …  Sl. No. …  Admission No. …` — Book No. + Sl. No. together are the tell of a **bound, serially-numbered TC book**. |
| Body | Numbered **1–22 with dotted leaders** to the right margin. **No table rules at all.** Multi-part items (6, 7, 11, 17) wrap to a second indented line. |
| Typography | Serif (Times) throughout, ~11 pt. Caps only for the title. **No bold anywhere.** |
| Ornament | None in the specimen. In practice *(common practice, not prescribed)*: single/double ruled border inset ~8–10 mm, screened crest watermark, guilloche edge on pre-printed stock. **Corner motifs not found on any TC** — they belong to award certificates. |
| Signature block | Three columns on one baseline, **no rules above them**: left `Signature of / Class Teacher`, centre `Checked by / (state full name and designation)`, right `Principal` with **`SEAL` on the line below**. The seal therefore lands **under the Principal, right column — not centred.** |
| Footer | **None.** No page number, no "computer generated" line, no QR. |

## 2. Transfer Certificate — STANDARD  **[measured]**

Source: Vidya Mandir Senior Secondary School, Vallanchery.

⚠ **Page is NOT A4:** 630 × 1026 pt (222 × 362 mm, foolscap/legal-ish) with a **9 pt bleed on all
sides** — i.e. commercially press-printed stationery, not desktop output. Side margins ~28 mm.

| Aspect | Finding |
|---|---|
| Letterhead | Circular crest **top-left**; school name to its right, ragged-left in heavy sans (~34 pt bold caps), sub-line ~20 pt; centred address (~13 pt bold), then `Tel : … E.mail : …` (~9 pt), then website. Closed by a **single thin full-width rule**. No affiliation number — this school is state-board. |
| Title | `TRANSFER CERTIFICATE`, centred, **bold sans**, caps, ~15 pt. |
| Serial block | On the **same baseline as the title** — `T C No` at the left margin, `Admission No.` at the right. **No Book No.**, no separate date field (date lives inside field 20). |
| Body | **Fully-boxed two-column label/value table**, 22 rows, hairline rules on all sides. Label column ~45%; value column empty for handwriting. Row heights vary with label wrap. Row 10 `Subjects studied :` lays six numbered slots **3 × 2 inside the label cell**. |
| Typography | Sans (Helvetica-like), labels ~9.5 pt regular; bold only on the title. |
| Ornament | **Deliberately none** — one rule, one table grid. No border, watermark or tint. |
| Signature block | Three labels on one baseline ~45 mm below the table, **no signature rules**: `Signature of Class Teacher` · `Prepared and Checked by` · `Principal`. **No explicit SEAL marker.** |
| Footer | None. |

### Pre-printed stationery — confirmed common

The bleed-marked non-standard sheet is press-run stock, and `Book No. / Sl. No.` implies bound
books with pre-numbered leaves.

- **Pre-printed:** letterhead, title, serial-block labels, all 22 field labels, table rules,
  signature labels, Book/Sl numbers
- **Filled:** every value, TC/Admission numbers, signatures, seal
- Counterfoil / duplicate-leaf practice: **not found** in a citable source

## 3. Bonafide — CLASSIC and STANDARD  **[reconstructed]**

⚠ **No primary school-issued PDF was reachable** (four sources 403'd, one TLS-expired). Layout
below is common practice reconstructed from format guides — **not prescribed, not measured.** The
prose sentences are verbatim from those guides.

**Classic:** A4 portrait, ~25 mm margins, on school letterhead. Ruled border inset ~10 mm and a
screened crest watermark are the usual "classic" cues; serif, body ~12 pt.
Title `BONAFIDE CERTIFICATE`, centred caps, **underlined** — underlining is near-universal here,
unlike the TC. `Ref. No. …` left / `Date: …` right, on one line **above** the title.
**No Book No./Sl. No.** — bonafides are not issued from bound books.
Body: one justified paragraph. **One signatory**, right-aligned `Principal`, round seal struck to
overlap the signature.

**Standard:** A4, ~20 mm margins, sans, body 11 pt, title 14 pt bold caps **not underlined**, one
rule under the letterhead, `Ref No.`/`Date` as a left/right pair beneath it. Body as three short
blocks: certifying sentence · supporting facts (DOB per school records, admission no., date of
admission) · purpose. One right-aligned `Principal` over a rule, seal beside.

**Verbatim sentences:**
1. "This is to certify that ____, son/daughter of Shri ____, is a bonafide student of this school
   and is studying in Class ____ during the academic year 2026-27."
2. "He/She has been enrolled in our institution since ____, and is currently studying in the
   10th Grade."
3. "This certificate is issued for the purpose of availing student concession on ____."

⚠ A verify-URL/QR footer: **no primary Indian school source confirming one was found.**

## 4. State-prescribed forms — where conduct actually lives  **[verified]**

| State | Prescribed form? | Conduct = separate cert or a field? |
|---|---|---|
| **Kerala** | **Yes — Form 5** (KER Ch. VI r.17(1)) | **Neither.** Form 5 has **no conduct field at all** |
| **Maharashtra** | **Yes — Appendix Four** (Secondary Schools Code r.32.1) | **Field** — item 8 `Conduct` (item 7 `Progress`) |
| **Karnataka** | In practice — SATS-generated, 25 fields | **Field** — item 25 `Character and Conduct / ನಡತೆ ಮತ್ತು ಚಾರಿತ್ರ್ಯ`, bilingual |
| **Delhi** | **No form prescribed** — DSER r.139 requires a TC but prescribes no format | — |
| **Rajasthan** | Not statutory | **Separate** Character Certificate via Shala Darpan (format unverified) |
| AP · Telangana · UP · West Bengal · Punjab | **Not found** | — |

### Two findings that overturn assumptions

**Kerala does NOT prescribe a Conduct Certificate.** Form 5 was read in full — its 23 fields
contain no conduct or character entry, and no KER rule creates such a certificate. Kerala schools
do issue one, but it appears to be **custom, not law**. (Maharashtra's Appendix Four and
Karnataka's SATS form are where conduct is actually prescribed — as a *field*.)

**No state prescribes grading vocabulary.** Where conduct appears it is an **unconstrained
free-text field**. "Good / Very Good / Excellent" is convention, not regulation — so a fixed
dropdown is a **product choice (Level D)**, and must not be presented as compliance.

Maharashtra also prescribes, verbatim, that duplicates are marked **"Duplicate" in red ink**
(r.30) and that there are only **two grounds for refusing a leaving certificate** — unpaid dues
and rustication (r.29.1).
