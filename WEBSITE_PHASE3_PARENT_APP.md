**Q. Is the app free for me?**
Yes. Always. Your school pays the subscription to use the [Brand] platform; you download the parent app at no cost. There are no in-app purchases or premium tiers for parents.

**Q. My school doesn't use [Brand] yet. What do I do?**
Ask your principal or admissions office to enable it. We can help — fill out our "Tell us about your school" form and we'll reach out to the institution directly. Once your school onboards, you'll get an SMS invitation to download.

Yes. Most schools enable separate logins for each linked parent or guardian. Some schools enable shared logins. Check with your school's office; we support both models.

**Q. What about grandparents or other guardians?**
Most schools allow linked guardian profiles in addition to parent profiles. This is configured by your school. Speak with the office to add a grandparent or other authorised guardian.

**Q. Will I get too many notifications?**
We engineered against this from day one. Notifications are categorised, you can silence categories you don't care about, low-priority updates are bundled into a daily digest, and quiet hours (10 PM – 7 AM) are respected.

**Q. What happens if I change my phone or reinstall the app?**
Sign in with the same credentials. Everything — your child's data, your message history, your receipts — is restored. The app is cloud-based; nothing important lives only on your device.

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (encrypted disk storage). Your school's data is isolated from every other school. Card payments are processed by Razorpay under PCI-DSS compliance — neither we nor your school ever see raw card data.

No. You see only your own child's data. Other parents see only their own children. Privacy is at the foundation of the platform.

**Q. What if my child changes schools?**
Your account remains accessible for historical records. Your new school may or may not use [Brand] — if they do, you can be added as a parent on the new school's instance.

**Q. What if the internet is down?**
The Parent App is offline-first. Already-loaded data — attendance, homework, marks, receipts — remains visible even without a connection. New data syncs the moment you come back online.

**Q. Why is the app Android-only right now?**
The vast majority of devices in our target markets are Android. We're building the iOS version for late 2026.

**Q. How do I delete my account?**
You can request account deletion from within the app's Settings → Account → Request Deletion. We process deletion requests within 30 days. Some data (financial records, audit trail) is retained for statutory compliance even after account deletion, as required by law.

# Cross-Section Design & Tone Notes

These notes keep the Parent App page emotionally consistent across all 21 sections.

## Tone Pillars

1. **Warm, not corporate.** Use "your child," "we," "you" liberally. Avoid "the parent," "the user," "the institution."
2. **Specific, not generic.** Real situations — meetings, traffic, school buses, dinner-table conversations — not abstract "challenges."
3. **Reassuring, not pressuring.** Don't manufacture parental guilt. The app is a tool for *being more present*, not for compensating for *being less present*.
4. **Honest about boundaries.** Acknowledge that the app isn't a replacement for being there; it's a way to bridge the gap.
5. **Premium, not corporate.** Even though the tone is warm, the visual treatment, photography, and copy quality should feel premium — this builds trust.

## Visual Style

- **Photography:** Real, candid, warm — commissioned, not stock. Parents in real environments (kitchen, office, car), interacting with the app naturally.
- **Color palette:** Warm neutrals with a primary accent (suggested: a warm teal #0F766E or muted gold #B45309) and secondary support colors. Avoid stark blues and greys — they feel clinical.
- **Typography:** A friendly serif for headlines (gives editorial warmth) paired with a clean sans for body. Consider Tiempos Headline + Inter, or GT Sectra + GT Walsheim.
- **Phone mockups:** Real product screenshots, drop-shadowed, slightly tilted for visual rhythm. No flat over-stylised mockups.

## Mobile Considerations

- The Parent App page itself will be read on phones more than any other page on the site.
- Make sure phone mockups inside sections shrink properly on mobile screens — they shouldn't dominate the screen on small viewports.
- CTAs should sticky-pin on mobile scroll for the "Download" action.
- Section padding should reduce significantly on mobile (60px instead of 120px) to keep the page from feeling endless.

## Conversion Funnel

Parents arrive at this page in three modes:
1. **Curious (came from social or search):** Looking to understand what the app does. The hero, daily visibility, and notifications sections answer their core question.
2. **Investigating (their child's school just adopted [Brand]):** Looking for setup help and reassurance. The privacy, FAQ, and support sections answer them.
3. **Ready (they have the SMS invitation):** Just want to download. The hero CTA, app store section, and final CTA strip all surface direct download links.

Make sure all three funnels are served — the page should not require linear reading.

## SEO Heading Strategy

- **H1:** The main hero headline. One only.
- **H2:** Each section headline (Sections 4–18).
- **H3:** Sub-headings within feature sections.

URL slug suggestion: `/parent-app` or `/products/parent-app`

Page meta title suggestion: "[Brand] Parent App — Your Child's School Day, Live in Your Pocket"

Meta description suggestion: "Stay connected to your child's school in real time. Attendance, homework, marks, fees, transport, and direct chat with teachers — on one app. Free for parents."
