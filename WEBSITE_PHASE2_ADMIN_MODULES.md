# Module 1 — Admission Management

**Eyebrow:** ADMISSIONS

**1. Marketing Headline:**
**Turn every enquiry into a decision — not a dropped lead.**

**2. Subheadline:**
A full-funnel admissions workspace that converts walk-ins, phone calls, web forms, and walk-aways into measured, managed, and accountable enrolment outcomes.

**3. Description:**

Admissions is the most measurable revenue function in any institution — and the most poorly instrumented. Enquiries live in a notebook. Follow-ups happen on someone's personal phone. Conversion rate is a guess. Counsellor performance is a feeling.

[Brand]'s Admission Management module replaces this with a proper CRM-grade pipeline built for educational institutions. Every enquiry is captured, scored, assigned, followed up, and measured — from the first phone call to the issued ID card.

When an enquiry converts, the entire admission record — personal details, academic history, documents, fee plan — flows automatically into the Student Lifecycle module. No re-entry. No re-uploading. No reconciliation.

**4. Pain Points We Eliminate:**

- Enquiries logged in registers, WhatsApp messages, and counsellors' personal phones — half of them forgotten by the next week.
- No standardised follow-up cadence. Some leads called five times, others zero.
- Conversion rate impossible to calculate because the denominator is unknown.
- Counsellor accountability based on memory, not data.
- Admission form filled at enquiry stage, refilled at admission stage, then refilled at student-profile creation. Three times the data entry, three times the typos.
- Document collection chased by phone, with no record of what's pending versus received.
- Offer letters drafted manually in Word with merge errors.
- Batch admissions during the season collapse into chaos as counsellors compete for the same prospective student.

**5. Key Capabilities:**

### Multi-Channel Enquiry Capture
- Public-facing web enquiry form (embeddable on any website)
- Walk-in entry by reception with single-screen capture
- Bulk import of enquiries from existing CRMs, Excel, or third-party lead vendors
- WhatsApp Business API integration (roadmap)

### Lead Scoring & Assignment
- Auto-scoring based on grade applied for, distance from campus, sibling status, board preference
- Round-robin or weighted assignment to counsellors
- Re-assignment on inactivity threshold

### Follow-Up Workflows
- Configurable follow-up cadences per stage (Enquiry → Counselling → Document Submission → Offer → Admission)
- Tasks auto-generated and routed to the assigned counsellor
- Missed-follow-up alerts to the admissions head
- Per-counsellor activity logs

### Conversion Pipeline
- Visual Kanban view of every enquiry by stage
- Bulk-stage transition (move 50 enquiries to "Document Pending" in one action)
- Configurable stages per institution

### Document Collection
- Required-document checklist per program/grade
- Parent upload portal with pending-document email reminders
- Internal verification workflow before admission can be confirmed

### Offer Management
- Templated offer letters with merge fields
- Branded PDF generation
- Email delivery with read receipts
- Accept/decline tracking with reasons

### Conversion to Enrolled Student
- One-click conversion from confirmed admission to active student record
- Auto-generation of student ID, roll number, fee plan, attendance roster placement
- Parent app provisioning triggered automatically

**6. Benefits:**

- **Measurable conversion rate per source, counsellor, and grade level** — replace gut feel with operating numbers.
- **Faster intake processing** — typical institutions move from 3-day-per-admission turnaround to 30 minutes.
- **Zero duplicate data entry** between enquiry, admission, and student profile.
- **Counsellor accountability dashboards** that surface activity, conversion, and pipeline health.
- **Audit trail of every interaction** — invaluable when parents dispute "what was promised."

**7. Cross-System Connection:**

- **Admin Panel:** primary workspace for the admissions team.
- **Parent App:** once admitted, parents are auto-provisioned and receive a welcome SMS with credentials.
- **Teacher App:** new students appear in the class roster automatically at session boundary, no manual addition.

# Module 2 — Student Lifecycle (SIS)

**Eyebrow:** STUDENT INFORMATION SYSTEM

**1. Marketing Headline:**
**One record. Every chapter. From admission to alumni.**

**2. Subheadline:**
A unified student information system that captures the complete academic, behavioural, financial, and operational history of every student — across every year, every class, every event.

**3. Description:**

Most institutions hold student data in fragments. Personal details in the admission form. Marks in an exam Excel. Fees in another system. Attendance in a register. Disciplinary notes in the counsellor's drawer. Behavioural observations in the class teacher's memory.

When a parent asks for "everything about my child," the office assembles a half-hour Frankenstein of attachments. When a transfer certificate is requested, someone manually pulls four years of data from four places.

[Brand]'s Student Lifecycle module is the antidote: a single, unified, lifetime record per student. Every interaction — admission, promotion, attendance, marks, fees, behaviour, transport, hostel, library, certificates, events, alumni status — flows into one auditable profile.

- Student data scattered across the admission file, the fee software, the marks spreadsheet, the attendance register, and the principal's emails.

- Transfer certificate generation takes hours of manual data collation.
- Alumni records lost the year after graduation.

### Behavioural & Wellness

### Operational

- Lost & found records

### Lifecycle Transitions
- Class promotion (batch and individual)
- Section transfer with audit trail
- Stream/elective switching
- Transfer Certificate (TC) generation with one-click PDF
- Alumni conversion with permanent record retention

### Document Vault
- Birth certificate, Aadhaar, previous-school transcripts, medical records
- Per-document expiry tracking and renewal reminders
- Cross-tenant isolation guarantees

**6. Benefits:**

- **One source of truth** for every student fact, every interaction, every transaction.
- **Faster TC generation** — minutes instead of hours.
- **Auditable promotion** — every batch promotion logged with actor and rationale.
- **Family-aware discounts** automatically applied when siblings are admitted.
- **Alumni records preserved** indefinitely — useful for placement, reunions, fundraising.
- **Parent-facing transparency** — Parent App reads from this same record.

# Module 4 — Timetable Management

**Eyebrow:** TIMETABLE & SCHEDULING

**1. Marketing Headline:**
**The schedule that knows your school.**

**2. Subheadline:**
A constraint-aware timetable engine that respects teacher availability, room capacity, subject preferences, and pedagogical best practices — and rebuilds itself when reality changes.

**3. Description:**

Building a school timetable is one of the most thankless tasks in administration. A senior staff member spends three weeks every summer in front of an Excel grid, trying to satisfy thirty teachers, twenty classes, eight subject-period requirements, four labs, and one principal who keeps changing the constraints.

And then the moment it's published — it breaks. A teacher resigns. A new section is added. A lab becomes unavailable. The timetable becomes a stack of marked-up PDFs.

[Brand]'s Timetable module reframes the problem. Configure your constraints once. Generate timetables in minutes. Adapt them in seconds. And publish them — live and synchronised — to every teacher and every parent.

**4. Pain Points We Eliminate:**

- Three-week annual timetable construction marathons in Excel.
- Teacher double-bookings that surface only on day one of term.
- Lab and room conflicts caught by a student saying "ma'am, the room is locked."
- No way to test a "what-if" scenario (what if Mrs. Sharma takes Thursdays off?).
- Substitute slot allocation invisible from the timetable view.
- Parents and teachers reading different versions of the timetable.
- Changes published as photographed PDFs on staff WhatsApp.

**5. Key Capabilities:**

### Drag-and-Drop Builder
- Visual grid by class, by teacher, or by room
- Drag a subject-period into a slot; conflict-check happens instantly
- Color-coded validity (green: valid, amber: warning, red: conflict)

### Constraint Engine
- Teacher availability (working days, daily start, daily end)
- Subject-period minimums per class per week
- Lab/room capacity and equipment requirements
- Pedagogical rules (no Maths after lunch in Grade 1; double-period blocks for labs)
- Custom rule definitions per institution

### Conflict Detection
- Real-time double-booking detection across teachers and rooms
- Subject-period shortfall alerts
- Workload imbalance warnings (a teacher with 38 periods/week)

### Multi-Section, Multi-Stream Support
- Stream-specific timetables (Science, Commerce, Arts) without duplication
- Elective-aware scheduling (students from different sections converge for electives)

### Substitute View
- One-click view of "free teachers right now"
- Substitute slot assignment integrated into the timetable

### Publishing & Sync
- Publish to teachers and parents with a single action
- Versioning with rollback
- Change diffs showing what's different from last week

### Auto-Generation (Roadmap)
- Constraint-satisfaction solver that drafts a timetable from your constraints
- Multiple candidates ranked by violation severity

**6. Benefits:**

- **Cut timetable construction time from weeks to days**.
- **Zero day-one conflicts** — caught at design time, not delivery time.
- **What-if scenarios** evaluable in minutes (what if we add a third Maths section?).
- **Live, synchronised distribution** — no more emailed PDFs.
- **Substitute decisions made from data**, not corridor conversations.

**7. Cross-System Connection:**

- Parents discover absences when the teacher calls in the evening — if at all.

### Parent Notifications
- Push notification on absence (configurable per institution)

### Late-Arrival Workflow
- Configurable tardy thresholds (8:00 AM cut-off)

- Habitual lateness flagging to the class teacher

### Analytics & Patterns
- Attendance heatmap per class
- At-risk student identification based on attendance drop patterns
- Day-of-week and seasonality analysis

### Compliance & Audit
- Locked monthly registers (no retroactive edits without audit log)
- Inspector-ready PDF exports
- Daily, weekly, monthly attendance reports

**6. Benefits:**

- **90-second class attendance** — reclaim 6+ minutes per period.
- **Real-time parent visibility** — no more discovery-by-phone-call.
- **Zero-effort monthly summaries** — auto-calculated, ready for report cards.

# Cross-Module Design Consistency Notes

These notes ensure all 13 module pages feel like part of one cohesive product family rather than thirteen disconnected pages.

## Layout Pattern (Reusable Template)

Every module page follows the same skeleton:

1. **Hero band** (above the fold) — Eyebrow + headline + subheadline + primary/secondary CTAs + product visual
2. **Pain-point strip** — 5–6 cards on a horizontal scroller
3. **Description / narrative** — 1–2 paragraphs of premium prose
4. **Capability grid** — Multi-column block of feature tiles
5. **Workflow visualisation** — Animated or static cross-system flow diagram
6. **Benefits / outcomes** — Outcome-driven bullets or stat strip
7. **Cross-system callout** — How this module interacts with Teacher / Parent apps
8. **Trust / proof** — Testimonial, customer logo, or measurable outcome
9. **Final CTA strip** — Primary + secondary + tertiary CTA
