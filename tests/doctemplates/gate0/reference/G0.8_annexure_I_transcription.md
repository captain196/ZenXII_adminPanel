## Signature block (verbatim layout)

```
   Signature of              Checked by                                 Principal
   class teacher             (state full name and designation)          SEAL
```

Three signatories. **SEAL** sits with the Principal.

## Countersignature rule (footnote, verbatim substance)

> Transfer certificate should be issued only under the signatures of the regular Principal/Vice
> Principal and it should be counter-signed by an officer not below the rank of District Inspector
> of Schools / Deputy Director of Education / Education Officer of the Education Deptt. of the
> State/Union Territory concerned.
>
> In case of a student migrating from one CBSE affiliated school to another CBSE affiliated school
> the transfer certificate … may be countersigned by the Regional Officer of the Board or the Asstt.
> Commissioner of the KVS or the Deputy Director, Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti … and by the First
> Secretary/Attache/Cultural Attache or an equivalent officer of the Embassy/High Commission of
> India … in respect of students studying in an affiliated school of the CBSE situated outside the
> country **and the student shall not be admitted to a school without such a counter signature.**

Amendment provenance: *"Rule amended in the Examination Committee's meeting of 7.5.1999 and
approved by the Governing Body at its meeting held on 13.5.1999."*

## ⚠ Corrections to the earlier research summary

The plan was right to forbid building the form from the summary. The summary was substantially
accurate but **imprecise in exactly the ways that matter for a prescribed form**:

| # | Summary said | Source actually requires |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | "DOB per Admission Register" | **figures AND words** — two inputs |
| 7 | "last class" | **figures AND words** |
| 10 | "subjects" | **exactly 5 numbered slots** |
| 11 | "promotion eligibility" | eligibility **plus** target class in **figures AND words** |
| 12 | "dues paid upto" | a **month**, not a date |
| 19–20 | "dates of application and issue" | **two separate fields**, 19 and 20 |

Field count confirmed at **22**.

## ⚠ Currency caveats — must be resolved before this profile ships

1. **Edition.** This is the **1995 edition updated to December 2004**. Research established a
   **January 2013 consolidated edition** exists with later amendment notifications. Fields
   themselves are unlikely to have changed, but this must be checked against the current edition.
2. **The countersignature footnote is partly superseded.** CBSE circular
   `Coord/ROs/Admission-IX-XI/2019` dated **18.07.2019** **removed** the countersignature
   requirement for **CBSE→CBSE** transfers. The non-CBSE-origin leg survives. The profile must
   model countersignature as **conditional**, not blanket.
3. **RTE override.** For classes I–VIII, RTE s.5(3) makes the TC immediate and non-withholdable;
   bye-law 8(iii)'s "no admission-register entry without TC" precondition is operative in practice
   for classes IX–XII only.

## Compliance-profile mapping (input to P1.8 / P5.1)

- `requiredKeys` = the 22 fields above. Sub-parts (figures/words, 5 subject slots) are **structure
  within a field**, not extra required keys.
- `requiredSignatures` = `class_teacher`, `checked_by`, `principal`
- `sealRequired` = true
- `stationery.preprintedBookSerial` = true (Book No. / Sl. No. / Admission No.)
- `countersignature` = conditional — required when origin board ≠ CBSE; **not** for CBSE→CBSE
  since the 2019 circular
- Two fields are **computed, not typed**: #14 total working days and #15 days present (Attendance
  module); #11 promotion eligibility (Result module)
- **#12 "dues paid upto" is rendered, and grants NO gating power** — see the no-dues legal position
  (ADR §9A.2)
