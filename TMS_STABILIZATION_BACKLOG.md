- **RC-5 — Role session-key drift (`role` vs canonical `admin_role`).** Drives: SEC-4 (dead admin endpoints).

## CRITICAL

### FIN-1 — Paid transport fee demands destroyed / money silently re-charged  `[verified: orchestrator]`
- **Where:** `Fee_lifecycle.php:776-778` (unconditional `paidAmount=0/status=unpaid`) defeats guard at `Fee_firestore_txn.php:913-922`; demand-id omits route (`Fee_lifecycle.php:755`); re-invoked at `Transport_service.php:2596, 2849, 3108, 3198`.
- **Scenario:** Student pays ₹2,000 transport → admin changes their route (or edits the assignment, or rollover runs) → the same deterministic demand doc is rewritten with `paidAmount=0, status='unpaid'`. Payment erased; student re-billed the full new amount; defaulter reports and Parent app show phantom dues.
- **Fix:** `createModuleFee` must not send `paidAmount/balance/status` for an existing demand (read-first or drop the keys so the BUG-075 guard preserves state); route changes adjust `grossAmount/netAmount` and recompute `balance = net − paidAmount`.
- **Verifier gate:** double-call `createModuleFee` after a payment → `paidAmount` and `status='paid'` intact.

### LOG-1 — Unfit / on-leave driver dispatched onto a live trip  `[verified: orchestrator]`
- **Where:** `_validateDriverReadiness` wired only at `Transport_service.php:3555, 3898`; **not** in `createTrip` (`:5626`), `startTrip`, or `dispatchEventTrip`. Repo's own `Transport_probe_f7.php:320-321` admits it.
- **Scenario:** Driver assigned in January (valid then); by July the licence/medical is expired or `operational_status='On_Leave'`/HR `Inactive`. `createTrip` resolves `getEffectiveDriver()` with zero re-validation and dispatches. The daily-mutable readiness state it exists to gate is never consulted at dispatch.
- **Fix:** call `_validateDriverReadiness($driverStaffId, $tripDate)` in `createTrip`/`dispatchEventTrip`/`startTrip`; validate the temp-driver window at `effective_to` too.
- **Verifier gate:** dispatch with an on-leave/expired/inactive effective driver throws and writes no `tripLogs`.
